Islam Balochi — Privacy Policy

App: Islam Balochi (اسلام بلوچی) · Package: com.islambalochi.app · Effective date: 3 November 2026

Islam Balochi is a free, non-commercial Islamic app (Quran with Balochi translation and tafsir, hadith, duas, prayer times, Qibla). It is developed by Hafiz Atta-ur-Rehman Aaskani. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. We do not collect personal data

  • The app has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any personal information.
  • Your settings (language, colours, prayer settings, last-read position) are stored only on your phone.

2. Location

  • With your permission, the app uses your device location only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • To show the name of your area (for example “Khalifa City, Abu Dhabi”), the coordinates are sent once to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service, which returns the place name. Nothing else is sent with them.
  • Your location is saved only on your phone. You can deny or withdraw location permission at any time in your phone settings.

3. Audio and internet

  • Quran recitation and Balochi translation audio are downloaded from public audio servers (such as everyayah.com, cdn.islamic.network and GitHub). Like any website, these servers can see your IP address when a file is downloaded. Downloaded audio is kept in the app’s cache on your phone.

4. Notifications and alarms

  • The app schedules local notifications on your phone for the adhan, morning/evening adhkar reminders and (in Ramadan) suhoor reminders. These are created on your device; no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app or in phone settings.

5. Questions you send (“Ask a question”)

  • The question form only prepares an email. It opens your own email app, and nothing is sent unless you press Send yourself.
  • The details you choose to write (name, father’s name, phone, email, area, country and your question) reach us by email and are used only to answer your question. They are not published or shared.
  • You may ask us to delete your message at any time by writing to us.

6. Sharing images

  • When you share a verse, hadith or dua as an image, the image is created on your phone and shared through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp).

7. Children

The app contains general religious content and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
