What these files are
The audio files in this repository and its Releases are recorded in the voice of
Hafiz Ata ur Rahman Aaskani. They include:
The Balochi translation and tafsir (commentary) of the Holy Quran in audio form
(translation and tafsir text by Sheikh Abdul Ghaffar Zamrani),
Adhkar, duas and the method of prayer with their Balochi translation,
Other Islamic audio prepared for the Islam Balochi app (اسلام بلوچی).
These files are hosted here only for use in the Islam Balochi app.
They are publicly reachable for technical reasons only; this does not give anyone
permission to use them.
