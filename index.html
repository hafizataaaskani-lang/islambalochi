Islam Balochi — اسلام بلوچی

A free Islamic app for Balochi speakers — no ads, no login.

  • Complete Quran with the Balochi translation and tafsir of Sheikh Abdul Ghaffar Zamrani, with recitation and Balochi audio translation
  • Hadith in Balochi: Forty Hadith of Imam Nawawi, Umdat al-Ahkam
  • Duas and morning/evening adhkar
  • Prayer times with adhan, Ramadan suhoor reminders, and Qibla
  • Verse and hadith of the day, shareable as images
